Email open tracking in a CRM: follow up at the right moment

Knowing whether a customer opened your email changes how you follow up. Here is how email tracking works and how to use it well.

By Vetra CRM14 November 20263 min read

How email open tracking works

When you send an email from a CRM, it can include a tiny tracking image. When the recipient's email app loads images, the CRM records the email as opened. Your team sees each email as seen or unseen.

Some email apps block images or load them automatically, so treat open tracking as a strong signal rather than proof.

How to use open tracking in sales

  • Call soon after a quotation email is opened
  • Resend or switch to WhatsApp if an important email stays unseen
  • Notice repeated opens; they often signal active interest
  • Compare open rates of different subject lines

Email templates that save time

Use built-in templates for replies, quotations and follow-ups, and create your own for messages your team sends often. Attach different sender email accounts to different templates, for example sales@ for quotations and support@ for tickets.

Tip

Keep templates short and personalise the first line with the customer's name and requirement.

Email campaigns

Schedule email campaigns for offers, product launches and reorder reminders. Track opens alongside replies and orders to see what actually drives sales.

Privacy and good practice

Use email tracking to improve timing and relevance, not to pressure customers. Send marketing emails only to people who have agreed to receive them, include an unsubscribe option and mention tracking in your privacy notice.

Subject lines that get opened

  • Be specific: “Quotation for 200 cartons – valid till Friday”
  • Use the customer's name or company
  • Keep it under about 50 characters
  • Avoid spam words and all-caps

Example: follow-up timing

A sales person sends a quotation email at 10 am. The CRM shows it was opened at 4 pm and again at 9 pm. The next morning the sales person calls, mentions the quotation and answers two questions. Without tracking, the call might have waited three days.

Frequently asked questions

Can a CRM tell me if a customer opened my email?

Yes. Vetra CRM shows whether each email sent was opened (seen) or not (unseen).

Is email open tracking 100% accurate?

No. Some email apps block or preload images, so open tracking is a useful signal but not exact.
