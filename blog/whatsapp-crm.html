What is a WhatsApp CRM?

A WhatsApp CRM connects your business WhatsApp number to your CRM through the WhatsApp Business API. Your team chats with customers from the CRM, and every message is saved against the right lead or customer record.

Problems with selling on a personal WhatsApp

  • Chats live on one person's phone
  • No way to see who replied to which customer
  • Customer history is lost when a sales person leaves
  • No link between the chat and the quotation, order or payment
  • Managers cannot see response times

WhatsApp Business App vs WhatsApp Business API

The free WhatsApp Business app works on one or a few devices and is fine for very small shops. The WhatsApp Business API lets software such as a CRM send and receive messages, so multiple team members can chat from one number, messages can be automated and campaigns can be scheduled. Business-initiated messages use pre-approved templates, and Meta charges for them based on message category.

What you can do with a WhatsApp CRM

  • Two-way chat with customers inside the CRM
  • Share product details, quotations and coupon codes in one click
  • Send order details, order status and delivery updates
  • Share support ticket status
  • Schedule WhatsApp marketing campaigns
  • Let an AI agent answer routine questions
Example

A buyer asks on WhatsApp, “Is my order dispatched?”. Your team opens the chat in the CRM, sees the order on the same screen and sends the tracking status without switching apps.

How to set up a WhatsApp CRM

  1. Get a WhatsApp Business API account through Meta or an approved provider.
  2. Verify your business with Meta.
  3. Connect the number to your CRM.
  4. Create and get approval for message templates.
  5. Train your team to reply from the CRM instead of their phones.

WhatsApp CRM in daily work

  • A new IndiaMART lead gets an instant WhatsApp template message; the sales person continues the chat from the CRM.
  • A buyer asks for a price; the sales person shares the product card and a quotation in the same chat.
  • A customer asks about delivery; the support person shares the order status and tracking without leaving the chat.
  • A customer reports a damaged product; a support ticket is created and its status is shared on WhatsApp.

Understanding the 24-hour customer service window

When a customer messages you, WhatsApp opens a customer service window during which you can reply freely. Outside that window, or when you start a conversation, you must use an approved template message. A CRM helps by showing whether the window is open and offering the right templates when it is not.

Managing a team on one WhatsApp number

With a WhatsApp CRM, several people can reply from the same business number. Assign chats to the lead owner, use internal notes for context and keep personal numbers out of customer conversations. When someone leaves the company, the chats stay with the business.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for a WhatsApp CRM?

Yes. To chat from a CRM with multiple team members, automate messages or run campaigns, you need the WhatsApp Business API connected to your number.
