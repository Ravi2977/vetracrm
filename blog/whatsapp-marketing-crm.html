WhatsApp marketing with a CRM: campaigns, templates and coupons

WhatsApp marketing works when messages are relevant, permitted and timed well. A CRM gives you the customer data to do all three.

Why run WhatsApp campaigns from a CRM

A CRM knows who your customers are, what they bought and when they last spoke to you. That makes WhatsApp campaigns targeted instead of mass broadcasts. Results also flow back into the customer record.

WhatsApp marketing rules to follow

  • Only message customers who have opted in to hear from you
  • Use templates approved through the WhatsApp Business API
  • Give customers an easy way to stop messages
  • Avoid sending too often; frequent messages lead to blocks and lower quality ratings

Campaign ideas that work

  • Festive and seasonal offers with a coupon code
  • Back-in-stock alerts for products a customer enquired about
  • Reorder reminders for consumable products
  • Follow-up after a quotation with a limited-time discount
  • Thank-you messages with a referral coupon after delivery

Scheduling and segmentation

Segment by lead source, product interest, purchase history or city. Schedule campaigns for times your customers usually reply, and avoid late nights. Combine WhatsApp with an email campaign for customers who prefer email.

Measure

Track replies, coupon redemptions and orders from each campaign, not just messages sent.

Writing WhatsApp templates that get approved and read

  • Keep the message short and specific: one offer, one action
  • Personalise with the customer's name and product of interest
  • Make the call to action clear: reply, view products or use a coupon
  • Avoid all-caps, too many emojis and misleading claims
  • Use the right template category, such as marketing or utility

Example campaign: reorder reminder

A distributor of cleaning supplies knows most shops reorder every 30 days. The CRM segments customers whose last order was 25 days ago and schedules a WhatsApp template on day 26: “Hi Ramesh, time to restock your floor cleaner? Reply YES to repeat your last order, or use code RESTOCK5 for 5% off this week.” Replies land in the CRM inbox and become orders.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Messaging everyone in your phonebook
  • Sending the same offer to every customer
  • Not tracking which campaign brought orders
  • Running WhatsApp campaigns from personal numbers

Combine WhatsApp with email

Some customers prefer email for detailed offers and catalogues. Use WhatsApp for short, timely messages and email for longer content. A CRM that runs both lets you see each customer's response across channels.
