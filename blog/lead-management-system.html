What is a lead management system?

A lead management system captures enquiries from every source, routes them to the right person, tracks their status and reminds your team to follow up until each lead is won or closed. It is usually the core of a CRM.

The 6-step lead management process

  1. Capture: pull leads automatically from IndiaMART, ads, website and WhatsApp.
  2. Acknowledge: send an instant reply so the lead knows you received it.
  3. Assign: route each lead to a sales person by round robin or rules.
  4. Qualify: confirm need, budget, quantity and timeline.
  5. Nurture: follow up with quotations, samples and reminders.
  6. Convert or close: record the order, or the reason the lead was lost.

Must-have lead management features

  • Multi-source lead capture
  • Instant notifications
  • Automatic email reply
  • Automatic assignment and visibility rules
  • Custom lead statuses
  • Full timeline of every action
  • Follow-up reminders
  • Reports by source, status and sales person

Lead statuses that work for most businesses

  • New
  • Contacted
  • Qualified
  • Quotation sent
  • Negotiation
  • Won / Order placed
  • Lost (with reason)
Keep it simple

Fewer, clearer statuses get updated more reliably than a long list nobody understands.

Common lead management mistakes

  • Leaving leads unassigned
  • No follow-up date on active leads
  • Not recording why leads were lost
  • Measuring lead count instead of conversions

Why speed to lead matters

Buyers often contact several businesses at once. The first business to respond with a useful answer has a strong advantage. An automatic acknowledgement plus a quick call from the assigned sales person can make the difference between winning and losing the order.

Simple lead qualification

You do not need complex scoring to start. Ask three questions: Does the lead need what we sell? Can they buy in the quantity or budget we serve? When do they plan to buy? Record the answers and focus your team's time on qualified leads first.

Lead management metrics to track

  • Leads by source
  • Average first response time
  • Contact rate (leads contacted / leads received)
  • Qualification rate
  • Lead-to-order conversion rate
  • Revenue by source and by sales person
  • Reasons for lost leads
