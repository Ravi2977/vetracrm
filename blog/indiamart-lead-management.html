How to manage IndiaMART leads: a practical CRM workflow

A simple process for keeping buyer enquiries organised, planning follow-ups and moving each opportunity towards a clear outcome.

IndiaMART can bring your business enquiries from buyers who are looking for products and suppliers. The next challenge is managing those conversations: who will respond, what the buyer needs, when to follow up and whether a quotation has been sent.

A consistent lead process gives each enquiry a place and a next action. If your IndiaMART lead source is connected to your CRM, new enquiries can arrive in the same workspace as leads from other connected channels.

Why a lead process helps

When enquiries are handled through separate inboxes, calls and spreadsheets, it can be difficult to see which buyers are waiting for a reply or which quotation needs a follow-up. A shared process helps a team review active opportunities without relying on one person’s memory.

A practical workflow for IndiaMART enquiries

1. Record the enquiry and its source

Keep the buyer’s contact details, business name, requested product and enquiry source together. If your IndiaMART connection is enabled, the lead can be captured in the CRM; otherwise, add the enquiry through your available lead-entry process.

2. Understand what the buyer needs

Record the product or service requested, quantity or scope, delivery location and any timing details the buyer shares. Add notes when a detail needs to be confirmed. This gives the next person context before they call or reply.

3. Choose a clear next action

Set a task to call, send product information or confirm a requirement. Add a due date and reminder so the enquiry returns to the team’s attention at the right time.

4. Move the opportunity through sales stages

Use stages that match your process, such as New Enquiry, Contacted, Requirements Confirmed, Quotation Sent, Negotiation and Won or Lost. Keep the stage current so you can see where work is waiting.

5. Send a product quotation and follow up

Prepare a quotation with the relevant products and send it by email or WhatsApp using your connected channel. Record when it was sent and review any quotation-view notification so your follow-up is timely and informed.

6. Track the sale after the quote

When an opportunity moves forward, keep product stock, invoice and payment status connected to the customer record. If it does not proceed, record the outcome and any useful reason rather than leaving the lead open indefinitely.

7. Keep after-sales work visible

For a completed order, the relationship may continue through support requests, returns or refunds. Keep those updates visible to the team so the buyer’s history does not end when the sale closes.

A weekly 15-minute lead review

Look at new enquiries, follow-ups due, quotations awaiting a response and opportunities with no next action. Assign a clear next step for each active lead.

Where a CRM fits
