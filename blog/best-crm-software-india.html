Why Indian businesses need a different kind of CRM

Many global CRMs were designed for email-first sales teams. In India, enquiries come from IndiaMART, JustDial, Google Ads and Facebook Ads, conversations happen on WhatsApp, and buyers expect a quotation and a GST invoice quickly. The best CRM for an Indian business needs to handle all of that without expensive add-ons.

10 features to look for

  1. Lead capture from your real sources: IndiaMART, Google Ads lead forms, Facebook lead ads and your website.
  2. Automatic first reply: an instant email reply to every new lead.
  3. Automatic lead assignment: round-robin or rule-based distribution to your team.
  4. Follow-up reminders: alerts before a follow-up is due.
  5. WhatsApp integration: two-way chat through the WhatsApp Business API.
  6. Quotations with payment links: so buyers can pay immediately.
  7. GST invoicing: invoices generated from the order.
  8. Inventory and orders: stock, purchases and delivery tracking.
  9. Roles and permissions: control who sees which leads.
  10. Reports and audit logs: visibility into team activity.

Questions to ask about CRM pricing

CRM prices in India vary widely. Before you compare numbers, ask:

  • Is the price per user or per account?
  • Is GST included?
  • Are WhatsApp, automation or AI features extra add-ons?
  • Is setup and onboarding included or charged separately?
  • Can I move to a higher plan later without losing data?

Cloud or self-hosted?

A cloud CRM is quickest to start. A self-hosted CRM runs on your own server and suits businesses that want full data control or a one-time license. Read cloud vs self-hosted CRM to decide.

How to test a CRM before you buy

  1. List your top three lead sources and ask the vendor to show those exact integrations.
  2. Run one real enquiry through the demo: lead, reply, follow-up, quotation, payment.
  3. Ask a sales person, not just the owner, to try it.
  4. Check how easy it is to export your data.
Tip

A CRM demo should use your workflow, not a generic sales script. Ask the vendor to show a lead from IndiaMART becoming a paid order.

Best CRM by type of business

  • IndiaMART sellers: prioritise IndiaMART lead import, instant alerts and auto replies.
  • Manufacturers: multi-product quotations, inventory and purchase management.
  • Distributors and wholesalers: WhatsApp orders, payment links and repeat-order campaigns.
  • E-commerce brands: order and delivery tracking, returns, refunds and a storefront API.
  • Service businesses: pipeline stages, follow-up reminders and quotations.

Red flags when choosing a CRM

  • The demo only shows dashboards, not a real lead becoming an order
  • WhatsApp is listed as a feature but needs a separate paid tool
  • No clear answer on data export if you leave
  • Pricing that changes depending on who you ask
  • No Indian-format invoicing or INR billing

Also ask who will help you set it up. A CRM that is half-configured will not be used, no matter how good its feature list looks.

A simple scoring sheet

Score each CRM from 1 to 5 on: lead capture from your sources, ease of use for sales people, WhatsApp, quotations and invoices, reports, support quality and total monthly cost. Weight the first two highest. The CRM with the best total usually wins, even if it is not the cheapest.
