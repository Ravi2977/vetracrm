Common challenges in distribution

  • Orders arriving on WhatsApp, calls and email
  • Checking stock before confirming every order
  • Chasing payments from many dealers
  • Remembering which dealer buys what and when
  • Field sales teams updating late

How a CRM helps distributors

  • One record per dealer with order history and outstanding payments
  • Stock levels visible to sales before confirming
  • Quotations and payment links on WhatsApp
  • Reorder reminders and offers by WhatsApp campaign
  • Delivery tracking shared with dealers

Grow repeat orders

Use purchase history to remind dealers before they usually reorder. Send targeted offers on slow-moving stock. Reward top dealers with coupons. All of this depends on clean data in one place.

Tip

Segment dealers by purchase frequency and send different campaigns to weekly, monthly and occasional buyers.

Example: an FMCG distributor

An FMCG distributor serves 300 retailers. Orders used to arrive on WhatsApp at all hours, and stock checks meant calling the warehouse. With a CRM, retailers' orders are logged against their records, stock is visible to sales, quotations go out with payment links and delivery status is shared on WhatsApp. Monthly reports show top retailers and slow-moving products.

Payments and credit

Distributors often extend credit. Record payment terms per dealer, link each payment to its order and review outstanding amounts weekly. Sending payment links with every quotation or invoice speeds up collection.

Purchases and stock

Use purchase management to record stock coming in from manufacturers and keep inventory accurate. When sales and purchases live in the same system, you can reorder before popular products run out.
