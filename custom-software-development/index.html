Why custom software

When ready-made software doesn't fit

Signs you need custom software

  • Work runs on Excel sheets and WhatsApp groups
  • Paying for 5 tools that don't talk to each other
  • Your process is unique and software forces you to change it
  • Owners can't see the numbers without asking someone

What custom software gives you

  • One system that matches your exact process
  • Tools connected through APIs
  • Automation for repetitive approvals and updates
  • Live dashboards for owners and managers

FAQ

Questions, answered

Ask a question
What kind of custom software do you build?

We build ERPs, customer and dealer portals, workflow automation, dashboards, integrations and SaaS products, for web, Windows, Mac, Android and iOS.

Who owns the software and source code?

You do. On final payment, the source code and all rights to your custom software belong to your business.

Can you integrate with Tally and other software?

Yes. We regularly connect custom software with accounting tools, payment gateways, WhatsApp, IndiaMART and other systems through APIs or data sync.

How do you price custom software?

After understanding your requirements, we send a fixed-price proposal with scope and milestones. Larger projects can be split into phases.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support plans covering bug fixes, updates, hosting and new features.

How long does a custom software project take?

A first working version usually takes 6 to 16 weeks depending on scope. We share a milestone plan with the proposal.
