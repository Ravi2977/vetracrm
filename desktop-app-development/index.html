FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our team.

Ask a question
Do you build desktop apps for both Windows and Mac?

Yes. We build for Windows, macOS, or both from one codebase, depending on your needs.

Can the desktop app work without internet?

Yes. We build offline-first apps with a local database, and add cloud sync when you need backups or multi-branch reports.

Can you connect printers, barcode scanners and other devices?

Yes. Desktop apps can work directly with thermal and A4 printers, barcode scanners, weighing scales and other hardware.

Can you upgrade our old desktop software?

Yes. We can rebuild older software, such as VB, FoxPro or Access applications, as a modern app and migrate your data.

How are updates delivered?

Apps can update automatically, so every machine stays on the latest version without manual installs.

How much does desktop software development cost?

Cost depends on features, integrations and platforms. Share your requirements and we'll send a fixed written quote.
