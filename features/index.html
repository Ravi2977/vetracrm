Lead capture from IndiaMART, Google Ads, Facebook Ads and your website

All plans

Every enquiry lands in one CRM, no matter where it comes from. Your team gets a notification and the customer gets an instant email reply that matches what they asked for.

  • IndiaMART lead integration

    Buyer enquiries from your connected IndiaMART account come into the CRM automatically, with the product and requirement attached.

  • Google Ads and Facebook / Meta lead forms

    Leads from connected Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead forms are added to your pipeline without copy-paste.

  • Custom pages and website forms

    Connect any landing page, website form or custom source to the CRM through the lead API or webhook.

  • Instant new-lead notifications

    The right people are alerted the moment a new lead arrives, so no enquiry waits.

  • Automatic email reply

    Each new lead gets an automatic email reply based on their requirement, even outside office hours.

  • Complete lead history and timeline

    Every status change, note, message, quotation and payment is saved in the lead's timeline.

  • Automatic order on payment

    When a payment is received, the CRM is notified, the order is placed and the lead is marked as converted.

  • Quotation viewed alerts

    Know when a customer opens the quotation so you can follow up at the right time.

  • Multiple payment gateways

  • Coupons and offers

    Create coupons and offers and share them with customers on email or WhatsApp.
