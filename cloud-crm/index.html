Why choose a cloud CRM?

A cloud CRM runs on the internet, so you do not need your own server, IT team or installation. Your sales team logs in from the office, from home or on the move, and every lead, chat, quotation and order is in the same place.

Online CRM for small businesses and growing teams

Most Indian SMEs get enquiries from IndiaMART, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, their website and WhatsApp. Vetra Cloud brings all of them into one pipeline, replies automatically, assigns each lead to the right person and reminds your team to follow up.

From enquiry to payment without switching apps

Send a quotation with a payment link. When the customer pays, Vetra is notified, the order is placed, the lead is updated and a GST invoice can be generated, so your team stops copying data between spreadsheets, WhatsApp and the payment gateway.

What is Vetra Cloud CRM?

Vetra Cloud is the hosted version of Vetra CRM. We run the CRM on our servers and your team gets its own login. There is nothing to install, and updates, hosting and backups are handled by us.

What is the difference between Starter, Growth and Pro?

Starter covers lead capture, the sales pipeline, follow-ups, quotations with payment links, orders, inventory and GST invoices. Growth adds automatic lead assignment, WhatsApp chat, campaigns, delivery tracking, returns and refunds, and custom roles. Pro adds the AI WhatsApp agent, the storefront API for your own online store, detailed reports and full system logs.

Which plan is best for IndiaMART sellers?

Starter is enough to capture IndiaMART leads, reply automatically and track every buyer to payment. If several sales people share leads or you sell on WhatsApp, Growth is the better fit.

How much does Vetra Cloud CRM cost?

Starter is ₹1,999 per month for up to 3 users, Growth is ₹4,999 per month for up to 10 users and Pro is ₹9,999 per month for up to 25 users. Prices are billed monthly and GST is extra. Setup and onboarding are optional. Need more users? Ask us for a quote.

Is there a Christmas offer on Vetra CRM?

Yes. Until 25 December 2026 you get 20% off Cloud plans and one-time setup: Starter at ₹1,599, Growth at ₹3,999 and Pro at ₹7,999 per month. Submit the form and our team will confirm the offer in your written quote.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can move from Starter to Growth or Pro as your team and requirements grow. We will confirm the change and price in writing.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

WhatsApp chat, WhatsApp campaigns and the AI agent need a WhatsApp Business API connection for your business number. We help you with the setup. Message charges from the WhatsApp provider are separate.

How does the AI agent work?

In the Pro plan you connect your AI provider's API key. The AI agent then replies to customers on WhatsApp about products, orders and support, shares order status, creates support tickets and hands the chat to a human when needed.

Can I move to my own server later?

Yes. If you later need the CRM installed on your own server, we can discuss moving you to Vetra Self-Hosted.
