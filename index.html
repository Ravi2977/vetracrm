Ask a question
Which software products does Vetra CRM offer?

Vetra CRM offers a CRM for lead-driven businesses, billing and invoicing software, medical store software, lab management software, hospital management software and inventory management software for electronics and other shops.

Is the software available on desktop, cloud and mobile?

Our products are available as cloud software, and billing and other products are also available as desktop software. Availability on Windows, Mac, Android and iOS depends on the product, and we confirm the right setup for your business in the demo.

Do you build custom software, websites and apps?

Yes. We build custom software, business websites, Android and iOS mobile apps, and Windows and Mac desktop apps. We also handle deployment, maintenance and ongoing support.

Is Vetra a good CRM for IndiaMART sellers?

Vetra is built for lead-driven Indian businesses. When your IndiaMART account is connected, enquiries come into your pipeline with alerts so your team can follow up, send quotations and track each buyer through to payment.

What can the AI WhatsApp assistant do?

It can reply to customers on WhatsApp about enquiries, order tracking, returns and refunds, and support requests, and log issues in the CRM for your team. WhatsApp API setup is required for your business.

How does Vetra CRM pricing work?

Vetra Cloud CRM has three monthly plans: Starter at ₹1,999 (up to 3 users), Growth at ₹4,999 (up to 10 users) and Pro at ₹9,999 (up to 25 users), GST extra. Self-Hosted starts at ₹1,49,999 one-time. Other software products and development services are priced by quote.

What does the 20% Christmas offer apply to?

The offer gives 20% off Vetra Cloud CRM plans and one-time setup until 25 December 2026. Submit your details and our team will confirm the offer in your written quote.

What happens after I request a demo or quote?

Our team reviews your enquiry and contacts you to understand your business and requirements. We then show you the relevant product or plan the project, and share pricing.
