Ask us in a free demo ↗
What can I manage in Vetra CRM?

Vetra brings connected lead capture, a sales pipeline, reminders, product quotations, stock, invoices, customer messaging, payment tracking and after-sales work into one workspace.

Can Vetra capture IndiaMART, Google Ads and Meta Ads leads?

Leads from these sources can come into Vetra when the relevant connection is configured for your workspace. We’ll confirm the setup needed for your accounts during the demo.

Can I send quotations on WhatsApp or email?

Yes. You can prepare quotations with multiple products and send them using connected email or WhatsApp services. API/provider setup may be required for your business.

Does Vetra track stock, invoices and payments?

Vetra includes product and stock tracking, invoice generation and payment tracking through an integrated payment gateway. The exact connection and workflow can be reviewed during your demo.

How does pricing work?

CRM access is a monthly subscription. Setup and onboarding are optional one-time services. Share your business needs and team workflow so we can provide a quote for the relevant scope.

What does the 20% Diwali offer apply to?

The offer covers the monthly CRM subscription and one-time setup. Submit your details and our team will confirm validity, the discounted amount and applicable dates in your written quote. The offer is not applied automatically at checkout.

What happens after I request a demo?

Our team will review your enquiry and contact you to understand your business, lead sources and sales process. We’ll then walk through the relevant Vetra CRM workflow and discuss pricing.

Can I control access for multiple users?

Vetra supports multiple users with roles and permissions, activity logs and configurable password/OTP login options. We can discuss the setup that fits your team.
