CRM GUIDE FOR INDIAN BUSINESSES

A CRM that fits
how Indian sellers work.

Selling through IndiaMART, ads, WhatsApp and email means enquiries arrive from many places. Here is how a CRM helps, and what to look for.

A CRM for IndiaMART sellers

If you sell on IndiaMART, every buyer enquiry is a chance to win an order, and buyers may contact several suppliers. A CRM like Vetra brings connected IndiaMART leads into one sales pipeline, alerts your team when a new lead arrives, and keeps notes, tasks and reminders next to each buyer so follow-ups do not depend on memory.

WhatsApp and email CRM in one place

Most Indian businesses talk to customers on WhatsApp and email. Vetra lets you connect your email and WhatsApp APIs, use reusable templates, send quotations, run scheduled campaigns with coupons, and let an AI assistant answer common customer questions on WhatsApp, so customer communication stays connected to your sales work.

Quotation, invoice and payment tracking

A sale does not end at the quotation. Vetra helps you build multi-product quotations, track products and stock, generate invoices, follow payment status through an integrated payment gateway, and manage support requests, returns and refunds, so your team sees the full customer journey in one place.

What to look for in a CRM for your business

  • Connected lead sources such as IndiaMART, Google Ads and Meta Ads
  • Follow-up tasks and reminders your team will actually use
  • WhatsApp and email messaging with reusable templates
  • Quotations, stock, invoices and payment tracking in one workflow
  • Roles, permissions and activity logs for your team

COMMON QUESTIONS

Get your questions answered ↗
Is Vetra a good CRM for IndiaMART sellers?

Vetra is built for lead-driven Indian businesses. When your IndiaMART account is connected, enquiries come into your pipeline with alerts so your team can follow up, send quotations and track each buyer through to payment. We’ll confirm the setup for your account in the demo.

What can I manage in Vetra CRM?

Vetra brings connected lead capture, a sales pipeline, reminders, product quotations, stock, invoices, customer messaging, an AI WhatsApp assistant, payment tracking and after-sales work into one workspace.

What can the AI WhatsApp assistant do?

It can reply to customers on WhatsApp about enquiries, order tracking, returns and refunds, and support requests, and log issues in the CRM for your team. WhatsApp API setup is required for your business, and we’ll walk through it in the demo.

Do I need the WhatsApp API to use the WhatsApp features?

Sending WhatsApp messages and quotations from Vetra, and using the AI assistant, needs a WhatsApp API connection for your business. We’ll explain the setup during the demo.

Can Vetra capture IndiaMART, Google Ads and Meta Ads leads?

Leads from these sources can come into Vetra when the relevant connection is configured for your workspace. We’ll confirm the setup needed for your accounts during the demo.

Can I send quotations on WhatsApp or email?

Yes. You can prepare quotations with multiple products and send them using connected email or WhatsApp services. API/provider setup may be required for your business.

Does Vetra track stock, invoices and payments?

Vetra includes product and stock tracking, invoice generation and payment tracking through an integrated payment gateway. The exact connection and workflow can be reviewed during your demo.

How does pricing work?

CRM access is a monthly subscription. Setup and onboarding are optional one-time services. Share your business needs and team workflow so we can provide a quote for the relevant scope.

What does the 20% Diwali offer apply to?

The offer covers the monthly CRM subscription and one-time setup. Submit your details and our team will confirm validity, the discounted amount and applicable dates in your written quote. The offer is not applied automatically at checkout.

What happens after I request a demo?

Our team will review your enquiry and contact you to understand your business, lead sources and sales process. We’ll then walk through the relevant Vetra CRM workflow and discuss pricing.

Can I control access for multiple users?

Vetra supports multiple users with roles and permissions, activity logs and configurable password/OTP login options. We can discuss the setup that fits your team.
