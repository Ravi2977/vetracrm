COMMON QUESTIONS

Installation, servers, pricing and data ownership.

Ask us in a free demo ↗
What is a self-hosted CRM?

A self-hosted CRM is installed on a server that you own or rent, instead of the vendor's cloud. Vetra Self-Hosted runs on your infrastructure, so your customer data stays with you.

Which features are included in Vetra Self-Hosted?

Vetra Self-Hosted includes every feature of the Pro plan: lead capture, automatic assignment, WhatsApp CRM, the AI agent, quotations with payment links, orders and delivery tracking, inventory, GST invoices, campaigns, full logs and the storefront API.

Who installs the CRM on my server?

Our team installs and configures Vetra on your server, connects your lead sources and integrations, and trains your team. We share the server requirements before installation.

What server do I need?

You need a server you control, such as a cloud VPS or a server in your own data centre. We recommend the right size for your number of users and leads during the demo.

How much does Vetra Self-Hosted cost?

Vetra Self-Hosted starts at a one-time license of ₹1,49,999 plus GST, including standard installation on your server. Annual support and updates are optional at ₹29,999 per year. Custom development is quoted separately.

Who owns the data in a self-hosted CRM?

You do. The CRM and its database run on your server, and you control access, backups and security policies.

Can I use my own domain and branding?

Yes. The CRM runs on your own domain or sub-domain, such as crm.yourcompany.com.

Should I choose Cloud or Self-Hosted?

Choose Cloud if you want to start quickly without managing a server. Choose Self-Hosted if you need data on your own infrastructure, have compliance requirements, or want a one-time license instead of a monthly subscription.
