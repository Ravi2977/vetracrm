FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our team.

Ask a question
Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We usually build with a cross-platform framework so one codebase runs on both Android and iOS, and use native code where an app needs it.

Will you publish the app on Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We prepare the store listing and handle the publishing process. Developer accounts are created in your business name so you own the app.

How long does app development take?

A focused first version usually takes 6 to 12 weeks depending on features. We share a milestone plan in the proposal.

Do I get an admin panel?

Yes. Most apps include a web admin panel to manage users, content, orders and reports.

Who owns the source code?

You do. On final payment, the source code and store accounts belong to your business.

How much does a mobile app cost?

Cost depends on screens, features, integrations and platforms. Share your idea and we'll send a fixed written quote.
