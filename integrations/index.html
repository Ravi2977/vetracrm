Integration questions

Ask a question
Do I need any setup to connect IndiaMART or ad lead forms?

Yes. Each lead source is connected to your workspace using that platform's access or API settings. We help you set this up and confirm what's needed for your accounts during the demo.

Is the WhatsApp Business API included?

Vetra connects to your WhatsApp Business API number. The API account and message charges are provided and billed by Meta or an approved provider, and we help you with the setup.

Can you build an integration that isn't listed?

Yes. Our development team builds custom integrations with other software through APIs, webhooks or data sync. Share your requirement and we'll send a quote.

Can my website send leads into Vetra?

Yes. Any website or landing page form can send leads to Vetra through the lead API or a webhook.
