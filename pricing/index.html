Ask a question
Are prices inclusive of GST?

No. All prices are in INR and GST is charged extra as applicable.

Why do some products show 'Get a quote' instead of a price?

Billing, medical store, lab, hospital and inventory software are priced by version (desktop or cloud), number of users or locations, and the setup you need. We share a written quote after a short demo so you only pay for what you use.

How are websites, apps and custom software priced?

Development projects are priced on scope. After a discovery call we send a fixed-price proposal with milestones, so you know the total before work starts.

Is there a setup fee?

Setup and onboarding depend on the product and how much data needs to be imported. Any setup cost is shown clearly in your quote.

Do you offer annual maintenance (AMC)?

Yes. Support and maintenance plans are available for our products and for websites, apps and software we build or take over.

Can I try before I buy?

Every product starts with a free demo using your kind of data and workflow, so you can see exactly how it will work for your business.
