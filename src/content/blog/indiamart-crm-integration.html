Why connect IndiaMART to a CRM

IndiaMART buyers often send the same enquiry to several suppliers. The supplier who replies first with a clear answer often wins. When leads stay inside the IndiaMART seller panel or a phone app, they get missed during busy days, and nobody can see which enquiries became orders.

How IndiaMART CRM integration works

IndiaMART provides an option for sellers to send their enquiries to an external CRM using an API key from the seller account. Availability depends on your IndiaMART plan, so check your seller panel or ask your IndiaMART account manager. Once connected, new enquiries flow into the CRM with the buyer's name, contact details, product and requirement.

What should happen after a lead arrives

  1. The sales team gets an instant new-lead notification.
  2. The buyer receives an automatic email reply matching their requirement.
  3. The lead is assigned to a sales person automatically.
  4. The sales person calls or messages and records notes.
  5. A follow-up reminder is set for the next step.
  6. A quotation with a payment link is sent.
  7. When the buyer pays, the order is placed and the lead is marked as converted.

Tips to win more IndiaMART leads

  • Reply within minutes, not hours
  • Ask one or two qualifying questions: quantity, location, timeline
  • Send a quotation the same day where possible
  • Follow up at least twice before closing a lead as lost
  • Track which product categories bring the most orders

Manual vs automatic IndiaMART lead handling

  • Manual: someone checks the seller panel, copies the lead into Excel and forwards it on WhatsApp. Leads are delayed and some are missed.
  • Automatic: the lead is created in the CRM within moments, the buyer receives a reply, the sales person is notified and a follow-up is scheduled.

What data to capture from each IndiaMART lead

  • Buyer name, company and contact details
  • Product or category enquired
  • Quantity and unit
  • Delivery city and state
  • Enquiry message
  • Enquiry type and time received

Store these as fields, not just in a notes box, so you can filter and report on them later.

Reports every IndiaMART seller should check

  • Leads per week by product category
  • Average time to first response
  • Leads contacted vs not contacted
  • Quotations sent and conversion to orders
  • Revenue from IndiaMART vs other sources

These numbers show whether your IndiaMART subscription is paying off and where your team needs to improve.
