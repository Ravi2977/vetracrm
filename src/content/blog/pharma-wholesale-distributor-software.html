A pharma distributor's day looks very different from a retail chemist's. Hundreds of line items per invoice, schemes from multiple companies, credit for every retailer, and dispatch deadlines. Software built for a retail counter often struggles.

Bulk billing that keeps up with dispatch

Invoices with many lines need fast item entry, batch selection by expiry, and quick printing. Look for the ability to convert a salesman's order directly into an invoice.

Schemes, discounts and rate lists

  • Company schemes such as free quantity and percentage discounts
  • Party-specific rates and discounts
  • Clear margin view after schemes

Batch and expiry across a large stock

Distributors hold large quantities, so expiry risk is bigger. Batch-wise stock, expiry reports and near-expiry returns to companies are essential. See medicine expiry management.

Credit control

Set credit limits and days for each retailer, block or warn on overdue accounts, and review ageing weekly. More in tracking outstanding payments.

Salesman orders and routes

Salesmen visiting retailers can book orders on a mobile app instead of calling them in. Route and salesman-wise sales reports show who is performing. A field sales order app can connect directly to billing.

Returns and claims

Handle sales returns from retailers and expiry or breakage claims to companies, with credit notes tracked on both sides.

Checklist

Bulk billing, schemes, party rates, batch and expiry, credit limits, salesman orders, returns and claims, GST reports. If a demo cannot show all of these, keep looking.
