Under GST, a tax invoice is more than a bill. It is the document your buyer uses to claim input tax credit and the record you report in your returns. Missing or wrong details can delay payments and create mismatches later.

This guide lists the fields a GST tax invoice should contain. It is a practical summary, not legal advice, so confirm specific cases with your CA.

Supplier details

  • Your legal or trade name
  • Your address
  • Your GSTIN

Invoice number and date

Each invoice needs a unique, consecutive serial number for the financial year. It can be up to 16 characters and may include letters, numbers, hyphens (-) and slashes (/). Many businesses use a prefix with the year, such as INV/26-27/0142. The invoice date is also mandatory.

Recipient details

  • Buyer's name and address
  • Buyer's GSTIN, if the buyer is registered
  • For unregistered buyers above a certain invoice value, the delivery address with state name and state code

Item details

For every line item, show:

  • Description of goods or services
  • HSN code (goods) or SAC code (services)
  • Quantity and unit
  • Total value and any discount
  • Taxable value after discount

The number of HSN digits depends on your turnover. Currently, businesses up to ₹5 crore aggregate turnover show at least 4 digits on B2B invoices; above ₹5 crore, 6 digits.

Tax details

Show the GST rate and the tax amount separately:

  • CGST + SGST/UTGST for supplies within the same state or union territory
  • IGST for supplies to another state, and for exports and SEZ supplies

Also show the place of supply with the state name for inter-state supplies, and mention whether tax is payable on reverse charge.

Signature

The invoice should carry the signature or digital signature of the supplier or an authorised person. Electronic invoices generated by software and e-invoices with an IRN are handled under their own rules.

Tip

Set up your company details, GSTIN, state, HSN codes and tax rates once in your billing software. Every invoice after that follows the same correct format automatically.

Bill of supply vs tax invoice

Composition dealers and sellers of exempt goods issue a bill of supply instead of a tax invoice, because no GST is charged on it. Your billing software should let you choose the right document type.

Common GST invoice mistakes

  1. Charging IGST instead of CGST and SGST (or the other way round)
  2. Missing or wrong buyer GSTIN on B2B invoices
  3. Breaks or duplicates in invoice numbering
  4. Wrong HSN code or rate for an item
  5. Manual calculation errors in totals and round-off

GST invoice checklist

  1. Your name, address and GSTIN
  2. Unique invoice number and date
  3. Buyer name, address and GSTIN (if registered)
  4. Description, HSN/SAC, quantity, unit
  5. Value, discount and taxable value
  6. GST rate and CGST/SGST or IGST amounts
  7. Place of supply and reverse charge mention
  8. Signature
