Your website or app was built by a freelancer or small agency. Now they are not answering, and you need a change, a fix, or a renewal. Don't panic; most situations can be recovered.

Step 1: List everything you depend on

  • Domain name and registrar
  • DNS provider
  • Hosting or cloud account
  • Website admin or CMS login
  • Source code repository
  • Business email
  • App store developer accounts
  • Payment gateway, WhatsApp API and other integrations

Step 2: Find out who owns each account

Check renewal emails and invoices. Accounts registered with your company email and in your business name are easy to recover. Ones in the developer's name need their cooperation or a provider's support process.

Step 3: Recover access

  • Use "forgot password" on accounts tied to your email
  • Contact providers' support with proof of business ownership
  • Ask the developer politely, in writing, for transfers and credentials

Step 4: Secure everything

Change all passwords, enable two-factor authentication, remove old users and API keys, and store credentials in a password manager controlled by your business.

Step 5: Take a full backup

Back up the code, database and files immediately. See backup strategy.

Step 6: Get a technical audit

A new partner reviews the code, hosting, security and pending issues, then recommends whether to maintain, improve or rebuild.

Prevent it next time

  • Register every account in your business name and email
  • Get source code access as part of the contract
  • Keep a simple document listing all accounts and renewals
  • Choose a partner with a team, not a single point of failure
