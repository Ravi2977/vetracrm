An AMC (annual maintenance contract) is an agreement where a technology partner keeps your software, website or app running, secure and updated for a year, for a fixed fee.

What an AMC usually covers

  • Bug fixes: problems in the existing software
  • Security and platform updates: frameworks, plugins, server packages
  • Backups: scheduled and tested
  • Monitoring: uptime and error alerts
  • Support: help by phone, email or WhatsApp
  • Small changes: a set number of hours per month
  • Renewals: domain, SSL and hosting management

What is often excluded

  • Large new features or redesigns
  • Problems caused by changes made by someone else
  • Third-party fees (hosting, domains, paid plugins, API charges)

Questions to ask before signing

  1. What are the support hours and channels?
  2. What are response and resolution times for critical, high and normal issues?
  3. How many hours of changes are included, and do unused hours carry over?
  4. How often are backups taken, and when were they last tested?
  5. Who holds admin access, code and domain ownership? (It should be you.)
  6. What happens at the end of the contract?
Ownership first
