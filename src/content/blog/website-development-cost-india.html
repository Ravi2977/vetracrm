"How much does a website cost?" is like asking how much a house costs. It depends on size, design, materials and finishing. Here are the factors that drive website development cost in India, so you can compare quotes on equal terms.

1. Type of website

  • Landing page: a single page for an ad campaign or product
  • Business website: 5–20 pages explaining your company, products and services
  • Catalogue website: many product pages with enquiry forms
  • E-commerce store: cart, payments, orders and customer accounts
  • Web application: portals, dashboards and custom functionality

Each step up adds design, development and testing time.

2. Design: template or custom

A template is quicker and cheaper. A custom design matches your brand and is built around your customers, which usually converts better but takes longer.

3. Number of pages and content

More pages means more design and development. Who writes the content matters too: professional copywriting and product photography add cost but often make the biggest difference to enquiries.

4. Features and integrations

  • Enquiry forms connected to your CRM
  • WhatsApp chat buttons
  • Payment gateway
  • Booking or appointment systems
  • Multi-language support

5. SEO and performance

A site built with clean structure, schema markup and fast loading costs a little more upfront but saves money on ads later. See SEO basics for small business websites.

6. Hosting, domain and maintenance

These are recurring costs: domain renewal, hosting, SSL and updates. Ask whether the quote includes the first year and what renewal costs. Read our website maintenance checklist.

Compare quotes properly

Ask each vendor for the same list: number of pages, design approach, features, content, SEO work, timeline, hosting, revisions and post-launch support. Only then compare prices.

Red flags

  • No written scope
  • The domain registered in the developer's name, not yours
  • No access to the website admin or code after launch
  • No mention of mobile performance or SEO basics
