The problem with managing sales on WhatsApp and Excel

Updates arrive late or not at all, follow-ups depend on memory, and managers only learn about lost deals after the fact. When leads are shared over WhatsApp groups, it is unclear who owns which customer.

What a CRM shows a sales manager

  • Leads assigned to each person and their status
  • Follow-ups due today and overdue
  • Quotations sent and viewed
  • Orders and payments received
  • Activity logs of calls, notes and messages

A simple daily CRM routine for sales people

  1. Start the day with today's follow-ups.
  2. Reply to new leads first.
  3. Update the status after every call.
  4. Add a note and set the next follow-up before moving on.
  5. Send quotations from the CRM so views are tracked.

How to get your team to actually use the CRM

  • Keep statuses and required fields minimal
  • Make the CRM the only place leads are assigned
  • Review the pipeline in weekly meetings using the CRM screen
  • Recognise people who keep records up to date
Manager tip

If it is not in the CRM, it did not happen. Consistency matters more than features.

A weekly sales meeting using the CRM

  1. Review new leads by source and how quickly they were contacted.
  2. Go through quotations sent and viewed but not yet paid.
  3. Check overdue follow-ups by person.
  4. Discuss lost leads and their reasons.
  5. Agree priority actions for the coming week.

Setting fair targets

Use CRM data to set targets based on reality: leads received, typical conversion rate and average order value. Track activity (calls, follow-ups, quotations) as well as results, so you can coach people who are working hard but converting less.

Field sales and remote teams

Field sales people should update the CRM from their phone right after a meeting: notes, next step and follow-up date. With activity logs and lead timelines, managers can see progress without daily calls.
