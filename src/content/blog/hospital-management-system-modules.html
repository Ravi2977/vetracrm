A hospital management system (HMS) connects the departments of a hospital so patient information, orders and charges flow from one place to another without paper files. Here is what each common module does.

Patient registration

Creates a unique patient ID with demographics and contact details. Every later visit, test and bill links to this record.

OPD and appointments

Appointment booking, token generation, doctor queues and consultation billing. See how to reduce OPD waiting time.

IPD, admissions and bed management

Admission, bed and ward allotment, transfers, nursing notes and daily charges for admitted patients.

Billing

OPD bills, IPD interim and final bills, packages, advances, discounts, refunds, and insurance or TPA details. Read the IPD billing process.

Pharmacy

In-house pharmacy with batch and expiry tracking. Medicines issued to admitted patients are added to their bills automatically.

Laboratory and radiology

Doctors order tests; samples and results are tracked; reports attach to the patient record.

EMR / patient records

Visit history, vitals, diagnoses, prescriptions and documents in one patient file accessible to authorised staff.

Discharge summary

Templates for discharge summaries that pull in diagnosis, procedures and medication.

Stores and inventory

Consumables and non-pharmacy items, purchase orders and department issues.

MIS and dashboards

Collections, occupancy, doctor-wise and department-wise reports for management.

Users and access

Role-based access so doctors, nurses, front desk, billing and admin see only what they need.

Where to start

Small hospitals usually see the fastest benefit from registration, OPD, billing and pharmacy. Add IPD and lab once the team is comfortable.
