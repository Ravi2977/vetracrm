In a retail shop, billing is the moment customers judge you. A slow counter means queues and walk-outs. Mistakes in price or stock cost money quietly every day. The right billing software fixes both.

Here are the features that matter most for grocery, electronics, mobile, hardware, garment, stationery and general stores.

Barcode billing at the counter

Scanning is faster and more accurate than searching. Your software should support standard barcode scanners and let you print barcode labels for items that do not have one. Weighing items and loose goods should be billable by weight or quantity.

Speed: keyboard shortcuts and quick search

Staff should be able to bill without touching the mouse: shortcuts for new bill, payment mode, discount and print. Search should work by part of the name, item code or brand.

Multiple payment modes

Customers pay by cash, UPI, card or a mix. Record split payments correctly so your day-end cash and UPI totals match.

Discounts, schemes and price lists

  • Item-level and bill-level discounts with owner control
  • MRP and selling price per item
  • Separate price lists for retail and wholesale customers

Returns and exchanges

Returns should add stock back and adjust the bill or issue a credit note, with a clear record of who processed it.

Live stock and reorder alerts

Every bill should reduce stock instantly. Reorder levels tell you what to buy before shelves run empty. For a deeper look, see how to calculate reorder levels.

Customer credit (udhaar)

Many retail customers buy on credit. Track each customer's balance, record payments and share statements on WhatsApp. More in tracking outstanding payments.

Day-end and owner reports

  • Day-end summary by payment mode
  • Item-wise and category-wise sales
  • Fast and slow-moving items
  • Profit by item and by day
Example

A mobile accessories shop scans a charger and a phone cover, applies a 10% combo discount, takes ₹300 cash and the rest by UPI, and prints a GST bill, all in under 30 seconds. At closing, the owner sees exactly how much cash should be in the drawer.

Staff permissions

Counter staff should bill and accept payments. Deleting bills, changing prices and giving large discounts should need owner permission.
