Hospital management software is a long-term decision. It will be used by doctors, nurses, front desk, billing, pharmacy and lab staff every day. Here is a checklist to compare options fairly.

1. Modules you need now and later

List the modules you need today (for example registration, OPD, billing, pharmacy) and those you may need later (IPD, lab, radiology, stores). Make sure the software can add them without starting over. See HMS modules explained.

2. Ease of use

Ask front desk and billing staff to try the demo. If they find it confusing, adoption will suffer no matter how many features it has.

3. Deployment: cloud or local

Consider your internet reliability, IT support and data policies. Some vendors, including Vetra, offer both cloud and local server options.

4. Data security and access

  • Role-based access for each type of user
  • Audit trails for important changes
  • Backups and a tested restore process

5. Billing depth

Check packages, deposits, refunds, insurance/TPA, and automatic posting of pharmacy and lab charges. Read IPD billing without revenue leakage.

6. Reports

Collections, occupancy, doctor-wise and department-wise reports should be available without exporting to Excel.

7. Integration

Ask about lab analyzers, accounting exports, SMS/WhatsApp, and any existing systems you want to keep.

8. Implementation and training

Who configures tariffs, doctors and formats? How is each department trained? Is there go-live support on site or remotely?

9. Support after go-live

Hospitals run around the clock. Clarify support hours, response times and how updates are delivered.

10. Total cost

Compare licence or subscription, setup, training, hardware, support and future modules over three to five years.

Do a reference check

Ask the vendor for a similar-sized hospital you can speak to, and ask what went well and what didn't during go-live.
