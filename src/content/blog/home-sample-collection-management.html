Home sample collection has become a standard expectation for many patients, especially the elderly and busy professionals. For labs, it brings extra revenue but also extra complexity: bookings, routes, staff, payments and sample integrity.

1. One booking channel, one schedule

Bookings arrive by phone, WhatsApp and the website. Put every booking into a single schedule with date, time slot, address, tests and patient contact. A booking form on your lab website can feed this directly.

2. Assign by area and time slot

Group bookings by area and assign them to collection staff so travel time stays low. Share the day's list with each staff member on their phone.

3. Prepare the right kit

Each visit needs the right tubes for the tests booked, labels, and a transport container. Checklists prevent repeat visits for a missing tube.

4. Record collection and payment

Record collection time and payment (cash or UPI) at the patient's home. Sharing a digital receipt builds trust.

5. Track samples back to the lab

Log receipt time at the lab and check labels against the booking. Barcodes help, as explained in sample tracking with barcodes.

6. Deliver reports digitally

Home-collection patients should never need to visit for a report. Send it on WhatsApp the moment it is approved.

Metrics to watch

Bookings per day, on-time arrival rate, repeat visits, and time from collection to report.
