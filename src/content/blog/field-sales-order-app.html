In many distribution businesses, salesmen visit retailers, write orders on paper or WhatsApp, and call them into the office. Orders get lost, schemes are applied wrongly, and managers have no view of the day's visits. A field sales order app solves this.

Core features

Retailer list and beat plans

Each salesman sees the retailers on today's route, with contact details and location.

Catalogue with stock and price

Products with images, prices, current schemes and available stock, so salesmen don't promise what isn't there.

Fast order taking

Add items quickly, apply schemes automatically and confirm the order with the retailer.

Outstanding and collections

See the retailer's outstanding before taking a new order and record payments collected.

Offline mode

Save orders without signal and sync later.

For managers

  • Visit check-ins with time and location
  • Orders and collections by salesman and route
  • New retailers added in the field

Connected to the office

Orders should flow into billing or ERP automatically, ready to invoice and dispatch. This is where most of the time savings come from.

Typical results

Distributors moving from phone orders to an app usually report fewer order errors, faster dispatch and a clear view of salesman performance.
