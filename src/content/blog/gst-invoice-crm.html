Why connect invoicing to your CRM

Your CRM already knows the customer, the products, the quantities and the agreed price from the quotation. Generating the GST invoice from the same order avoids copying data into separate billing software and keeps sales and accounts in agreement.

What a GST invoice needs

  • Your business name, address and GSTIN
  • Customer name, address and GSTIN (for B2B)
  • Invoice number and date
  • HSN or SAC code for each item
  • Taxable value and the correct CGST/SGST or IGST breakup
  • Place of supply

Rules such as e-invoicing apply to businesses above turnover limits set by the GST authorities. Confirm your obligations with your CA.

The quote-to-invoice flow

  1. Quotation is sent with a payment link.
  2. Customer pays and the order is placed automatically.
  3. GST invoice is generated from the order.
  4. Invoice is stored in the CRM media library and shared with the customer.
  5. Inventory is updated.

Keep inventory in sync

When invoicing and inventory sit in the same system as sales, stock levels update as orders are placed. Sales people can see what is available before they promise delivery.

Good practice

Keep product HSN codes and tax rates in the product master so every invoice is correct by default.

B2B and B2C invoices

For B2B sales, include the buyer's GSTIN so they can claim input tax credit. For B2C sales, the buyer's GSTIN is not required, but place of supply still decides whether CGST and SGST or IGST applies. Keeping customer type and state in the CRM helps get this right on every invoice.

Common invoicing mistakes a CRM helps avoid

  • Wrong tax type because of incorrect place of supply
  • Missing or wrong HSN codes
  • Prices on the invoice that do not match the quotation
  • Duplicate invoice numbers
  • Invoices not shared with the customer or not stored

Storing and sharing invoices

Store each invoice against the order and customer in the CRM's media library. Share it on email or WhatsApp directly from the CRM, so both your team and the customer can find it later without searching through chats.

Can a CRM generate GST invoices?

Yes. Vetra CRM generates GST invoices from orders, using product and customer details already in the CRM.

Do I still need accounting software?

Many businesses keep accounting software for books and returns, while using the CRM to generate invoices from sales. Ask your CA what fits your business.
