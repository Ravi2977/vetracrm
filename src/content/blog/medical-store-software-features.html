A medical store handles thousands of items, each with batches, expiry dates, MRPs and schemes. Customers expect quick service and the right substitute when a brand is unavailable. Doing this on paper or in general billing software leads to slow counters and expired stock.

Here are the twelve features that matter most when you choose medical store software.

Billing and counter

1. Fast medicine search and billing

Search by brand name, first few letters, or barcode, and bill strips or units with GST calculated automatically. Keyboard shortcuts keep the queue moving.

2. Batch-wise billing

When an item has several batches, the software should suggest the batch closest to expiry (first-expiry, first-out) so older stock sells first.

3. Salt or composition search

When a brand is out of stock, staff should find substitutes with the same composition in seconds.

4. Customer and doctor details

Save regular customers and prescribing doctors to speed up repeat purchases and keep a useful record.

Stock and expiry

5. Expiry alerts

See medicines expiring in the next 30, 60 or 90 days so you can sell, return or exchange them before they become a loss. Read our guide on managing medicine expiry.

6. Reorder and short lists

Generate a short list from minimum stock levels and recent sales, then send orders to distributors.

7. Expiry and breakage returns

Create supplier returns for expired or damaged stock and track credit notes from distributors.

Purchases and suppliers

8. Purchase entry with batch, MRP and scheme

Enter purchase bills with batch, expiry, MRP, purchase rate, free quantity and discount. Your margins are only as accurate as this entry.

9. Supplier outstanding

Track how much you owe each distributor and record payments.

Reports and control

10. GST and sales reports

Sales, purchase and GST reports for your CA, plus daily collection by payment mode.

11. Margin and stock value reports

Know which items and companies make you money, and how much cash is tied up in stock.

12. User permissions and backups

Staff should bill without changing rates or deleting bills. Regular backups protect years of data.

Demo test

Ask the vendor to enter one purchase bill with two batches, then bill the item and show which batch was picked. Then ask for the 60-day expiry report. This single test shows whether the software was built for pharmacies.

Desktop or cloud?

Single-counter chemists often prefer desktop software that keeps working without internet. Pharmacies with several branches benefit from cloud reporting. Vetra Medical Store Software is available in both versions.
