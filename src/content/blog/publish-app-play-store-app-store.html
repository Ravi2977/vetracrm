Publishing an app involves accounts, listings, policies and reviews by Google and Apple. Knowing the steps in advance avoids launch-day surprises.

1. Create developer accounts in your business name

  • Google Play Console: one-time registration fee (US$25 at the time of writing) and identity verification
  • Apple Developer Program: annual membership (US$99 at the time of writing); organisations need a D-U-N-S number

Owning these accounts means you own the app, even if you change development partners.

2. Prepare the store listing

  • App name, short and full description
  • Icon, screenshots and optionally a promo video
  • Category and contact details

3. Privacy and data disclosures

Both stores require a privacy policy URL and a description of what data the app collects and why. Be accurate; mismatches can cause rejection.

4. Test before release

Use internal and closed testing on Google Play and TestFlight on iOS. Test on a range of real devices, including budget Android phones popular in India. New personal Google Play accounts may have additional testing requirements before production release.

5. Submit for review

Common rejection reasons include crashes, broken links, missing login details for reviewers, unclear permissions and incomplete metadata. Provide a test login for reviewers if your app requires sign-in.

6. Release gradually

Staged rollouts release the update to a percentage of users first, so you can catch problems early.

7. Keep it updated

Stores regularly require apps to target newer OS versions. Plan regular updates, and monitor crash reports and reviews.
