Every business eventually asks: should we buy software that already exists, or build our own? Both are valid. The right answer depends on how standard your process is and how important it is to your competitive edge.

When off-the-shelf software is the right choice

  • Your process is standard: billing, accounting, basic CRM, payroll
  • You need to start quickly
  • The software already does 80–90% of what you need
  • Your budget is limited

When custom software makes sense

  • Your process is unique and is part of why customers choose you
  • You are stitching together several tools with Excel and WhatsApp
  • Per-user subscription costs grow painfully as you scale
  • You need deep integration with existing systems or hardware
  • You want to own the software and its data completely

The hybrid approach

Many businesses use ready-made products for standard work, such as Vetra CRM or Vetra Billing, and build custom modules, portals or integrations around them for the unique parts.

A simple test

If you describe your process to three software vendors and all three say "you'll need to change the way you work", it may be time to consider custom software.
