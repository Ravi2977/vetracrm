In many OPDs, patients spend more time waiting than with the doctor. The delay is usually not the consultation itself but everything around it: registration, finding old records, billing and unclear queues.

1. Measure where time goes

Track time from arrival to registration, registration to consultation, and consultation to billing and pharmacy. You will usually find one or two clear bottlenecks.

2. Use appointment slots

Give patients time slots instead of asking everyone to come at 10 am. Leave some slots for walk-ins and emergencies.

3. Pre-register patients

Collect details when the appointment is booked, by phone, WhatsApp or online. Returning patients should be found instantly by mobile number.

4. Run a visible token system

Issue tokens per doctor and display the current token. Patients relax when they can see progress.

5. Keep records digital

Searching for paper files wastes minutes per patient. Electronic records let the doctor see history immediately.

6. Speed up billing and pharmacy

Collect consultation fees at registration and send prescriptions electronically to the pharmacy so medicines are ready.

7. Keep patients informed

A WhatsApp or SMS update when their turn is near lets patients wait comfortably instead of crowding the desk.

Example

A polyclinic moved to appointment slots with pre-registration and a token display. The front desk crowd disappeared, and doctors started consultations on time because patients arrived spread across the morning.
