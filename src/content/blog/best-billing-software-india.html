Billing software is the one tool most Indian shops and traders use every single day. A good one saves minutes on every bill, keeps GST clean for your CA and tells you who owes you money. A bad one slows down the counter and creates more work at month end.

This guide explains what to look for, so you can compare options with confidence instead of picking whatever a friend uses.

1. GST invoicing that is fast and correct

The basics are non-negotiable. Your billing software should create tax invoices with your GSTIN, HSN or SAC codes, and the right split between CGST and SGST for sales within your state or IGST for sales to another state. It should also handle B2B invoices (with the buyer's GSTIN) and B2C bills.

Speed matters just as much. Watch a demo and count the clicks for a normal bill. Search by item name or code, barcode scanning and keyboard shortcuts make a big difference during a rush. If you are unsure which fields an invoice needs, see our guide to the GST invoice format.

2. Stock that updates itself

When billing and inventory are separate, they never match. Choose software where every sale reduces stock and every purchase bill increases it. Look for:

  • Low-stock alerts and reorder levels per item
  • Purchase entry with supplier details and rates
  • Units and conversions (box, piece, kg)
  • Batch, expiry or serial number tracking if your trade needs it

3. Payments and outstanding

Many businesses sell on credit. Your software should record full and part payments against invoices and show each customer's outstanding balance in one screen. Reminders and the ability to share a statement on WhatsApp help you collect faster. Read more in how to track customer outstanding payments.

4. Desktop, cloud or both

This choice depends on how you work:

  • Desktop suits a busy counter with unreliable internet. It works offline and prints fast.
  • Cloud suits owners who want to check sales from home, or businesses with more than one location.

Some software, including Vetra Billing, offers both. Our desktop vs cloud billing comparison goes deeper.

5. Reports your CA can use

At return time you need sales and purchase registers, GST summaries by rate, and party-wise reports. Day book, profit and stock value reports help you run the business the rest of the month. Ask for sample reports in the demo and check they export to Excel or PDF.

6. Users, roles and safety

If staff bill for you, give them billing access only. Owners should control discounts, deletions, settings and reports. Regular backups (or cloud storage) protect years of data if a PC fails.

Quick test before you buy

Ask the vendor to set up ten of your real items and bill them in front of you, including a return and a part payment. You will learn more in fifteen minutes than from any feature list.

7. Setup, training and support

Software is only useful if your team actually uses it. Check whether the vendor imports your item list and opening stock, trains your staff, and answers on call or WhatsApp when something goes wrong. Local support in your language is worth paying for.

8. Pricing that fits

Compare the total cost over a year or two, not just the first payment: licence or subscription, number of users, setup, updates and support. Cheap software that needs workarounds every day is not cheap.

Checklist: the best billing software for you

  1. Creates correct GST invoices quickly, with barcode support
  2. Updates stock with sales and purchases
  3. Tracks payments and outstanding by customer
  4. Works the way you work: desktop, cloud or both
  5. Gives reports your CA needs
  6. Has user permissions and backups
  7. Comes with setup, training and responsive support
