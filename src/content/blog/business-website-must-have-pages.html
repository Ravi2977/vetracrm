Visitors arrive with simple questions: What do you do? Can you help me? Can I trust you? How do I contact you? Each page of your website should answer one of these clearly.

Home page

State what you do and who you serve in one sentence at the top, followed by your main products or services, proof (clients, certifications, photos), and a clear call to action.

Product or service pages

Create one page per main product or service. Explain the problem it solves, features, who it is for, process, FAQs and a contact form. Separate pages also rank better on Google for specific searches.

About page

Your story, team, experience, facilities and certifications. People buy from people; real photos help.

Contact page

Phone, WhatsApp, email, address with a map, working hours and a short enquiry form. Send form submissions straight to your CRM so no enquiry is missed.

Pricing page (if possible)

Even "starting from" prices or "how pricing works" reduce unqualified enquiries and build trust.

Case studies or portfolio

Show real work with outcomes. Ask clients' permission before naming them.

Blog or guides

Helpful articles answer customer questions and bring search traffic over time. See SEO basics.

FAQ page

Answer the questions your sales team hears every day.

Legal pages

Privacy policy and terms, plus refund and shipping policies for e-commerce.

Navigation tip

Keep the main menu short: Products/Services, Pricing, About, Blog, Contact, plus one standout button such as "Get a quote".
