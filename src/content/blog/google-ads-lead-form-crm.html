What are Google Ads lead forms?

Lead form assets show a short form inside your Google Ads (Search, YouTube, Display and more). Users submit their details without visiting your website. Leads can be downloaded from Google Ads, or sent to a CRM in real time using a webhook.

How to connect Google Ads lead forms to your CRM

  1. Copy the webhook URL and key from your CRM.
  2. In Google Ads, open your lead form asset and find the lead delivery option.
  3. Paste the webhook URL and key.
  4. Send test data from Google Ads and check that a test lead appears in the CRM.
  5. Turn on alerts and assignment rules for new Google Ads leads.

Follow up fast

Google lead forms often capture people actively searching for a solution, so speed matters. Send an automatic email reply, assign the lead immediately and call within minutes during business hours.

Measure which campaigns bring sales

  • Record the campaign and keyword source on each lead
  • Track leads through quotation and payment stages
  • Compare cost per paying customer, not just cost per lead
  • Pause campaigns that bring leads but no orders
Tip

Add your website form to the same CRM so you can compare Google Ads lead forms with landing page enquiries.

Choosing lead form fields

Google lets you pick common fields such as name, email, phone, city and company, plus custom questions. Fewer fields usually mean more leads; one or two qualifying questions improve lead quality. Map each field to a matching CRM field so data lands in the right place.

Lead forms vs landing pages

  • Lead forms: fewer steps, more leads, especially on mobile; less information about your offer
  • Landing pages: more space to explain and qualify; slower, and some visitors leave
  • Best practice: run both, send both into the same CRM and compare conversion to paying customers

Example: industrial equipment supplier

An equipment supplier in Ahmedabad runs Search ads with a lead form asking for machine type and required capacity. Leads arrive in the CRM in real time with the campaign name. The CRM assigns them to the product specialist, sends an automatic email with the brochure and creates a follow-up for the next morning. Monthly reports show cost per paying customer by campaign, not just cost per lead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get Google Ads leads into my CRM automatically?

Use the webhook option on your Google Ads lead form asset and paste the webhook URL and key provided by your CRM.

Can I track which Google Ads campaign a lead came from?

Yes. Lead form data includes campaign details, which your CRM can store against the lead for reporting.
