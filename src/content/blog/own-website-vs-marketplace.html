If you sell products online in India, you will face this question early: sell on marketplaces like Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho, or build your own store? Each has clear strengths.

Marketplaces: reach, with rules

  • Built-in traffic: millions of shoppers already searching
  • Trust and logistics: payments, delivery and returns handled
  • Fees: commissions and other charges reduce margin
  • Competition: your product sits next to competitors, often compared on price
  • Limited customer relationship: you have little control over communicating with buyers directly

Your own website: control and loyalty

  • Your brand, your rules: design, pricing, offers and policies
  • Customer data: build a list for repeat purchases and WhatsApp marketing
  • Better margins: no marketplace commission
  • Traffic is your job: SEO, ads, social media and referrals

The practical answer: both

Use marketplaces for discovery and volume. Use your website for repeat customers, bundles, launches and full-price sales. Include a card in every marketplace order inviting customers to your website for reorders and offers.
