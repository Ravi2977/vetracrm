Search engine optimisation (SEO) helps your website appear when people search for what you sell. For a small business, the basics below cover most of what matters.

1. Know what customers search

List the words customers actually use: "steel pipe manufacturer in Pune", "pathology lab near me", "billing software for medical store". Use Google's search suggestions and "People also ask" to find more.

2. One page per main topic

Give each product, service or location its own page, focused on one main search phrase. A single page that tries to rank for everything usually ranks for nothing.

3. Titles, descriptions and headings

  • Title tag: the main keyword and your brand, around 50–60 characters
  • Meta description: a clear summary that makes people click
  • One H1 per page, with logical H2 subheadings

4. Fast, mobile-friendly pages

Most searches in India happen on phones. Pages should load fast and not jump around while loading. Read Core Web Vitals explained.

5. Structured data

Schema markup (organisation, products, FAQs, articles, breadcrumbs) helps search engines understand your content and can improve how results appear.

6. Google Business Profile

For local customers, claim and complete your Google Business Profile: category, hours, photos, products and regular posts. Ask happy customers for reviews.

7. Helpful content

Write guides that answer real customer questions. Each good article is a new way for people to find you.

8. Internal and external links

Link related pages to each other. Earn links from industry directories, associations, suppliers and local media.

9. Measure

Set up Google Search Console and Google Analytics to see which searches bring visitors and which pages generate enquiries.

Common mistakes

Copying text from other sites, stuffing keywords, slow image-heavy pages, missing contact details, and never updating the site.
