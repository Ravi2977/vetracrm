The reorder level is the stock quantity at which you place a new order. Set it right, and new stock arrives just before the old stock runs out.

The formula

Reorder level = (average daily sales × lead time in days) + safety stock

  • Average daily sales: how many units you sell on a normal day
  • Lead time: days between placing an order and receiving it
  • Safety stock: a buffer for busy days and late deliveries

Example

A shop sells about 8 phone chargers a day. The distributor delivers in 3 days. The owner keeps a safety stock of 10.

Reorder level = (8 × 3) + 10 = 34 chargers. When stock falls to 34, it's time to reorder.

Calculating safety stock

A common formula is:

Safety stock = (maximum daily sales × maximum lead time) − (average daily sales × average lead time)

If the shop sometimes sells 12 chargers a day and delivery sometimes takes 5 days:

Safety stock = (12 × 5) − (8 × 3) = 60 − 24 = 36. That is a more cautious buffer. Choose based on how costly a stock-out is for that item.

How much to order

The reorder level tells you when to order. To decide how much, consider how long the stock should last, supplier minimums, schemes and your available cash. Many shops set a maximum stock level and order up to it.

Practical tips

  • Use recent sales (last 30–90 days), not guesses
  • Set higher levels before festivals and seasonal peaks
  • Keep lower levels for slow or expensive items
  • Let software flag items below reorder level daily
