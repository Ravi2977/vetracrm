Ask patients what they want from a lab and "fast reports" comes up first. Doctors waiting to start treatment feel the same. Reducing turnaround time (TAT) is one of the best ways to win referrals.

1. Measure TAT first

You cannot improve what you do not measure. Record times at each step: collection, receipt in lab, result entry, approval and delivery. Your lab software should report average TAT by test and highlight delayed samples.

2. Remove registration delays

Pre-registration over the phone or WhatsApp, quick search for returning patients and test packages save minutes per patient at the front desk.

3. Label samples at collection

Barcodes printed at collection avoid relabelling and mix-ups. Samples move straight to the right department. See barcode sample tracking.

4. Plan runs and batches

Schedule analyzer runs around peak collection times so samples do not wait for the next batch. Separate urgent samples clearly.

5. Speed up result entry

Manual typing is slow and risky. Where possible, connect analyzers; otherwise use structured entry screens with reference ranges and auto-calculation.

6. Make approval easy

Pathologists should see a queue of reports waiting for approval, with abnormal values highlighted, and approve from any computer.

7. Deliver instantly

The moment a report is approved, send the PDF on WhatsApp or email. This alone can save hours compared with patients collecting printouts.

Quick win

Many labs find their biggest delay is not testing but the gap between approval and delivery. Automatic WhatsApp reports often cut perceived TAT dramatically.
