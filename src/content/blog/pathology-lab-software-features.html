A pathology lab runs on accuracy and turnaround time. Patients want reports quickly; doctors want them correct and readable. Lab management software, often called a LIS (Laboratory Information System), ties every step together.

Front desk

Patient registration

Register walk-in, referred and home-collection patients quickly. Returning patients should be found by mobile number with their previous visits.

Test and package billing

Select tests or packages, apply discounts, and record advance and balance payments. Rates for different referral sources or corporate tie-ups should be supported.

Samples

Barcode labels

Print barcode labels at collection so every tube is linked to the right patient and test. This is the single biggest protection against mix-ups. More in sample tracking with barcodes.

Worklists by department

Haematology, biochemistry and other sections should see their own pending samples.

Results

Result entry with reference ranges

Enter results against age and gender-specific reference ranges, with abnormal values highlighted automatically.

Approval before release

The pathologist reviews and approves reports before they reach patients.

Reports

Professional report formats

Your letterhead, signatures, method notes and clear layout. See how to design a lab report patients understand.

WhatsApp and email delivery

Send PDF reports as soon as they are approved, so patients and doctors do not have to wait or visit again.
