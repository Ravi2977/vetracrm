Small clinics and polyclinics often run on a register, a receipt book and memory. That works until the patient list grows, a second doctor joins, or a patient asks what was prescribed last time.

What clinic software does

Appointments

Book appointments by slot, see each doctor's day at a glance, and handle walk-ins.

Patient records

Each patient's visits, vitals, notes and prescriptions in one place, found by name or mobile number.

Prescriptions

Create clear, printed or shared prescriptions using templates and favourite medicines.

Billing and receipts

Consultation and procedure billing with receipts, and daily collection reports.

Reminders

Follow-up reminders on WhatsApp or SMS reduce missed visits.

Signs your clinic needs software

  • Patients wait while staff search for old records
  • You cannot tell how much was collected today without counting
  • Follow-up patients are forgotten
  • More than one doctor shares the front desk
