What is a cloud CRM and a self-hosted CRM?

A cloud CRM (also called SaaS or online CRM) runs on the vendor's servers. You log in through a browser and pay a monthly subscription. A self-hosted CRM (also called on-premise CRM) is installed on a server you own or rent, usually with a one-time license.

Side-by-side comparison

  • Setup time: cloud is ready in days; self-hosted needs a server and installation
  • Cost: cloud is a monthly fee; self-hosted is a one-time license plus optional yearly support
  • Maintenance: the vendor maintains cloud; your team (or the vendor's support) maintains self-hosted
  • Data location: vendor's cloud vs your own infrastructure
  • Customisation: self-hosted is usually easier to customise deeply
  • Domain: self-hosted can run on your own domain, e.g. crm.yourcompany.com

Choose cloud CRM if…

  • You want to start this week
  • You do not have an IT team
  • You prefer a predictable monthly cost
  • Your team is small or growing quickly

Choose self-hosted CRM if…

  • You need customer data on your own servers for policy or compliance reasons
  • You prefer to own the software rather than rent it
  • You have many users and want to avoid per-user monthly fees
  • You want deep customisation or integration with internal systems
Total cost over 3 years

Compare 36 months of subscription with a one-time license plus three years of support and server costs. For larger teams, self-hosted often comes out cheaper.

Is self-hosted more secure than cloud?

Not automatically. A self-hosted CRM is only as secure as the server it runs on. Cloud vendors handle updates and backups for you. Self-hosting gives you control, which helps when you have the people to manage it. Choose based on who will look after security day to day.

What you need for a self-hosted CRM

  • A server you control: a cloud VPS or a machine in your own data centre
  • Someone responsible for backups, updates and monitoring
  • A domain or sub-domain for the CRM
  • SSL certificate and basic firewall rules
  • A plan for support, either in-house or from the vendor
