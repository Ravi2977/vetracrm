The billing counter is where a medical store earns its reputation. Customers want accuracy and speed. Owners want every strip billed at the right price and stock to stay correct. A clear process helps your team do both.

Step 1: Read the prescription

Confirm the medicine name, strength and quantity. For prescription-only medicines, follow the rules that apply to your store and keep records as required.

Step 2: Search and add items

Search by brand or scan the barcode. Good software shows stock, MRP and available batches immediately.

Step 3: Let the software pick the batch

The nearest-expiry batch should be picked automatically. Staff should only override it with a reason.

Step 4: Offer substitutes when needed

If a brand is out of stock, use salt search to show alternatives, as appropriate and as permitted.

Step 5: Apply discounts carefully

Use preset discount rules rather than ad-hoc changes, and restrict large discounts to the owner.

Step 6: Take payment and print

Record cash, UPI and card correctly, including split payments. Print or share the bill on WhatsApp.

Step 7: Handle returns properly

Accept returns only against a bill, add stock back to the correct batch, and record the reason.

Step 8: Close the day

Match cash in the drawer and UPI receipts with the day-end report. Investigate differences the same day, while memories are fresh.

Common counter mistakes

Billing the wrong batch, selling loose strips without recording them, manual price changes, and returns without a bill. Software rules prevent most of them.
