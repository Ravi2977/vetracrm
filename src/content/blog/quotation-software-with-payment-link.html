Why quotations stall

Many businesses build quotations in Word or Excel, send a PDF on WhatsApp, then ask for payment by bank transfer in a separate message. The buyer has to switch apps, find your bank details and confirm the payment. Each extra step gives them a reason to delay.

How quotation with payment link works

  1. Create a quotation with multiple products, quantities and prices.
  2. Send it by WhatsApp or email directly from the CRM.
  3. Get notified when the customer views it.
  4. The customer pays through the payment link inside the quotation.
  5. The CRM receives the payment update, places the order and marks the lead as converted.

Benefits

  • Faster payments with fewer steps for the customer
  • No manual matching of bank transfers to quotations
  • Automatic order creation
  • Clear record of what was quoted and paid

Quotation best practices

  • Send quotations the same day as the enquiry where possible
  • Include validity dates and delivery timelines
  • Follow up soon after the customer views the quote
  • Offer a coupon for quick payment if margins allow
Tip

The best time to call is right after the CRM tells you the quotation was viewed.

Example: from enquiry to paid order in one day

A furniture supplier receives an enquiry for 20 office chairs at 10 am. By 11 am the sales person sends a quotation on WhatsApp with three chair options and a payment link. The CRM shows it was viewed at 1 pm, so the sales person calls and answers a question about delivery. The buyer pays the advance at 3 pm; the CRM places the order, updates the lead and notifies the dispatch team.

What every quotation should include

  • Your business details and GSTIN
  • Customer details
  • Product names, specifications and images where useful
  • Quantity, unit price, discounts and taxes
  • Delivery timeline and charges
  • Payment terms and validity date
  • A payment link for advance or full payment

Using multiple payment gateways

Some customers prefer UPI, some cards and some net banking. Different gateways also have different fees and settlement times. Being able to configure more than one gateway gives you flexibility and a backup if one is down.
