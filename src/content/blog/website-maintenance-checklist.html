Websites break quietly. A contact form stops sending emails, a plugin update fails, an SSL certificate expires. Customers notice before you do. A simple checklist prevents most problems.

Weekly

  • Submit a test enquiry through every form and confirm it reaches your inbox or CRM
  • Check uptime alerts and fix any downtime causes
  • Confirm backups ran successfully
  • Review any security alerts

Monthly

  • Update the CMS, themes, plugins and server packages (after a backup)
  • Check page speed and Core Web Vitals (see Core Web Vitals explained)
  • Look for broken links and 404 errors in Google Search Console
  • Review analytics: traffic, top pages, enquiries
  • Update products, prices, offers and team details

Quarterly

  • Test restoring a backup to a staging copy
  • Review user accounts and remove old logins
  • Refresh old blog posts and key landing pages
  • Check mobile layout on a few real phones

Yearly

  • Renew the domain (consider auto-renew and multi-year)
  • Confirm SSL auto-renewal works (see SSL certificate expired)
  • Review hosting plan and performance
  • Review privacy policy and legal pages
  • Plan design or content improvements
Most-missed item

Testing the contact form. A broken form looks fine but silently loses every enquiry. Test it weekly.
