Serialised inventory means tracking individual units, not just quantities. For mobiles, laptops, TVs, appliances and tools, this is the only way to answer questions like "Was this phone sold by us?" or "Which supplier did this faulty unit come from?"

How it works

  1. At purchase: scan or enter each unit's IMEI or serial number into the purchase entry.
  2. In stock: the software knows exactly which serials are available at each location.
  3. At sale: scan the unit's serial on the bill. The software confirms it is in stock and links it to the customer.
  4. After sale: search any serial to see its full history.

Benefits

  • Warranty: instant proof of purchase date and seller
  • Returns: return the exact unit to the right supplier
  • Fraud prevention: catch attempts to return a unit not bought from you
  • Audits: verify physical units against system serials

Tips for accurate tracking

  • Always scan rather than type serial numbers
  • Do not allow billing a serialised item without a serial
  • Check serials during stock transfers between locations
  • Audit high-value serials monthly
Example

A customer brings back a phone claiming it was bought last week. Staff search the IMEI and see it was sold eight months ago at another branch, with warranty still valid. The claim is handled correctly in minutes.
