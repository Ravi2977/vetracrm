Why cloud

Why choose a cloud CRM?

No server, no IT team

A cloud CRM runs on the internet, so you do not need your own server, IT team or installation. Your sales team logs in from the office, from home or on the move.

Online CRM for growing teams

Most Indian SMEs get enquiries from IndiaMART, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, their website and WhatsApp. Vetra Cloud brings them into one pipeline, replies automatically and assigns each lead.

Enquiry to payment, one app

Send a quotation with a payment link. When the customer pays, Vetra places the order, updates the lead and a GST invoice can be generated.
