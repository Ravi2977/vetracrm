Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens when you send an enquiry through the Vetra CRM website.

Information you submit

Our contact and offer forms may ask for your name, business name, work email, mobile number, city, state or region, business type, area of interest and message. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in the message field.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry, arrange a product demo, prepare a subscription, licence, setup or project quote, and follow up about the offer you requested.

Where the enquiry is stored

Website form submissions are sent to Vetra CRM so the team can review and respond to enquiries. A copy is also sent to the team's Google Apps Script and connected Google Sheet. Google processes and stores that copy under its own services and terms.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and whether a form was submitted.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the form. To ask us to correct or remove an enquiry you already sent, use the contact form and include the email address used for that enquiry.

Questions

For questions about a website enquiry, contact the Vetra CRM team.
